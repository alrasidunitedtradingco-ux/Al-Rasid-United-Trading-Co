### 🟠 PHASE 4: PAYMENTS (WEEK 4)

When you're ready to accept real money...

#### Set Up Stripe
```
1. Create Stripe account (5 min)
   Go to: https://stripe.com
   
2. Connect bank account (10 min)
   Provide bank details
   
3. Create payment button (20 min)
   Add to checkout page
   
4. Test payment (10 min)
   Use Stripe test card: 4242 4242 4242 4242
   
5. Deploy (1 min)
   Upload to Netlify

Total time: 1-2 hours
```

#### Payment Flow
```
Customer fills checkout form
    ↓
Clicks "Pay Now"
    ↓
Redirected to Stripe payment page
    ↓
Enters card details
    ↓
Payment processed
    ↓
Receipt email sent
    ↓
Money in your bank account (2-3 days)
```

#### Fee Structure
```
Card payments:
- 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction
- Example: $100 order → $3.19 fee

Bank deposit:
- Free
- Takes 2-3 days
- Automatic daily/weekly

No monthly fees!
```

#### Result
- ✅ Accept online payments
- ✅ Automatic receipts
- ✅ Professional invoices
- ✅ Money in your account
- ✅ Complete order tracking
